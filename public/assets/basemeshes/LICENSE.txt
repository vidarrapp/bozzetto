Base meshes
===========

The .bzm files in this folder are exported from the Human Base Meshes
bundle (v1.4.1) published by Blender Studio:

    https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/#asset-bundles
    https://developer.blender.org/docs/features/asset_system/asset_bundles/human_base_meshes/

The bundle is released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Made by Dan Ulrich (realistic bodies, heads, hands, feet, eyes, jaws),
Julien Kaspar (stylized figures and parts), Paul Kotelevets (planar and
generic heads, primitive figures, realistic skull) and Tonatiuh de San
Julián (skulls). Thank you.

The export (tools/export-basemeshes.py in the Bozzetto repository) keeps
the meshes as authored at one chosen subdivision level, turns Blender's Z
up into Y up, and voxel-remeshes the primitive figures into single shells.
The thumbnails are the bundle's own asset previews.
