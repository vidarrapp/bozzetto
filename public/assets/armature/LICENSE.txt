Mannequins
==========

The .glb files in this folder are the "primitive" figures of the Human
Base Meshes bundle (v1.4.1) published by Blender Studio, rigged onto
Bozzetto's own bones by tools/build-mannequins.py in the Bozzetto
repository:

    https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/#asset-bundles

The bundle is released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The figures were made by Paul Kotelevets and Julien Kaspar. Thank you.
