Terms of use

Version 2026-10-08. What you and Bozzetto can expect of each other, what may not be put here, and how to report what should not be.

The service

  • Bozzetto is free, and open by invitation. It comes as it is, with no promise that it will always work, or keep everything you put in it, so keep copies of what matters to you.
  • It may change, and it may end. If it ends, you will be told in good time to download your work first.
  • An account is for one person. Keep your sign-in to yourself.

Your work

  • What you make stays yours. By saving it here you let Bozzetto store it and show it back to you, and nothing more.
  • Nothing you make is shown to anyone else. Sharing your work in public, when it comes, will be your choice, piece by piece.
  • Templates are free to use as starting points for your own work, including commercial work. The template itself may not be redistributed as your own.
  • Each account has room for 250 MB. Files are checked as they arrive, and a file that is not what it says it is, is refused.

Content policy

Do not upload, make or share here:

  • anything illegal;
  • any sexual content involving minors. There is no tolerance for it at all, and it is reported to the police;
  • intimate images of anyone without their consent, harassment, threats or hate;
  • other people's work or likeness when you do not have the right to use it;
  • malware or files made to break something, spam, or anything that pretends to be someone it is not.

Artistic nudity in figure work is allowed. Sexual content involving minors, intimate images of anyone without their consent, and pornographic content are not.

Suspension and removal

An account, or work, that breaks these terms may be suspended or removed. You will be told why, and how to object if you think it was a mistake.

Reporting content

To report something on Bozzetto that you believe is unlawful, write to bozzetto@vidarrapp.se and include:

  • which project it is (its link);
  • why you believe it is unlawful;
  • how to reach you;
  • a statement that you are making the report in good faith, and that it is accurate as far as you know.

Reports are acknowledged within 7 days, and acted on sooner where a child's safety or a threat is involved. This is the notice and action that the EU's Digital Services Act asks of services that store what others put on them.

Law

These terms are under Swedish law. Nothing in them takes away your rights as a consumer.

Changes
